# Demo Script (about 5 minutes)

**1. Introduce (30 sec).** "In Karachi, booking a water tanker is done by phone. There is no fixed price, no tracking and no record. Our system, PaniGo, fixes this."

**2. Resident - English (90 sec).**
1. Step 1: tap "Big tanker".
2. Step 2: choose a district, then an area (for example East, Gulshan-e-Iqbal). Type a house address. Pick "Morning".
3. Tap "Check on Google Maps" to show the map opens. Optionally tap "Use my location".
4. Step 3: show the summary and total price. Tap "Book now". Show the booking card (status: waiting for a driver).

**3. Urdu mode (30 sec).** Tap the "اردو" button. "Many tanker users are not comfortable with English, so everything is in Urdu with big icons." Tap "A+" to show bigger text.

**4. Dispatcher (45 sec).** Open "Dispatcher". Show the stats, choose a driver, tap "Assign driver".

**5. Driver (45 sec).** Open "Driver", select the driver, tap "Open in Google Maps" to show navigation, then tap the delivered button.

**6. Back to Resident (15 sec).** The status now says "Water delivered" with a green bar.

**7. Show the code (60 sec).** Open `src/models`: abstract `User` and `Tanker`, private `#status` in `Booking.js`, `LargeTanker.getPrice()` override, role classes extending `User`.

**8. Close (30 sec).** Answer sir's 7 questions: problem, user, industry, solution, OOP, unique (Urdu, simple design, Karachi areas), demo done.

Tip: press "Reset demo" at the bottom before starting.
