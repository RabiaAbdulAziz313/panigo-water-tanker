The problem

Many areas of Karachi do not get regular piped water, so families depend on private tankers that are booked by phone. Industry: utilities and local public services.

No fixed price

Rates change from caller to caller.

No tracking

Residents do not know when the tanker will arrive.

No records

Orders get missed or duplicated.

No proof

Drivers get verbal instructions and delivery cannot be verified.

Our solution

One connected workflow for three roles: Resident, Dispatcher and Driver.

Book

The resident chooses tanker, place and time, and sees the exact price first.

Assign

The dispatcher sees every booking and assigns a driver in one click.

Deliver

The driver opens the location in Google Maps and marks the job delivered.
